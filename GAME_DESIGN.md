# CHRONOFALL — 4D Arena Shooter
## Master Game Design Document

---

## CONCEPT

CHRONOFALL is a 4D arena shooter that bridges the gap between Free Fire (battle royale tension), Mini Militia (fast pick-up-and-play), Call of Duty (tactical loadouts & competitive feel), and Poki (instant web access — no download needed).

### The "4D" Difference
The 4th dimension is **TIME**. Players can:
- **Rewind** their own position and health 3 seconds back (Q key)
- **Slow-Mo** time for everyone else while you move at normal speed (E key)
- **Chrono Echo** — leave a past version of yourself that fights alongside you for 5 seconds
- **Temporal Dash** — teleport 5 meters forward through time

*No other shooter on the market has time manipulation as a core combat mechanic. This is our unique selling point.*

## COMPLETELY NEW MECHANICS (Our Secret Sauce)

1. **Chrono Rewind** — Rewind your position, health, and ammo 3 seconds. Enemies freeze during rewind. Cooldown: 15s.

2. **Slow Motion Field** — Time slows to 20% for everyone except you. You become a blur. Duration: 4s. Cooldown: 20s.

3. **Chrono Echo** — Spawn a ghost copy of yourself from 5 seconds ago that fights with your last actions. Duration: 5s. Cooldown: 30s.

4. **Temporal Dash** — Instant teleport 5m in your movement direction. Leaves a visual trail. Cooldown: 8s.

5. **Time Fractures** — Cracks in the arena that act as portals — walk through one, come out another. Random placements each match.

6. **Phase Shift** — Briefly exist outside of time — can't take damage, can't deal damage. Good for dodging. Duration: 1.5s. Cooldown: 12s.

7. **Chrono Storm (Ultimate)** — Rewind the ENTIRE arena 10 seconds. Everyone goes back. Tactical mind games. Once per match.

8. **Echo Combat** — Killing an enemy creates a "time ghost" of them that fights for you for 10 seconds.

9. **Temporal Zones** — Map areas where time flows differently (faster or slower). Creates strategic positioning.

10. **Paradox Mode** — Special mode where two rounds play simultaneously — actions in round 1 affect round 2.

## GAME MODES

1. **Chrono Royale** — 30 players, shrinking temporal zone, last one standing
2. **Team Deathmatch** — 5v5, first to 50 kills, instant respawn
3. **Free-for-All** — 8 players, everyone for themselves, 10 min timer
4. **Search & Destroy** — Plant the "chrono bomb" or defend, no respawn
5. **Temporal Horde** — PvE waves of time-corrupted enemies, co-op
6. **Zone Control** — Hold temporal zones for points
7. **Local Rift** — Bluetooth/WiFi direct, up to 8 players, no internet needed
8. **Chrono Arena** — 1v1 duel mode, best of 5
9. **Paradox Mode** — Two simultaneous rounds, actions echo
10. **Training Grounds** — Practice against bots with adjustable difficulty

## TECH STACK

### Prototype (Current)
- HTML5 + JavaScript (vanilla)
- Three.js for 3D rendering
- Web Audio API for sound
- Browser-native (no engine dependency)

### Production (Target)
- **Game Engine:** Godot 4 (open source, mobile export, web export) or Unity
- **Backend:** Node.js + Socket.io for real-time multiplayer
- **Database:** MongoDB (player data, stats, cosmetics)
- **Authentication:** JWT-based
- **CDN:** Cloudflare for asset delivery
- **Mobile:** Capacitor wrapper for native app stores
- **PWA:** Service worker for offline single-player

### Why This Stack
- Godot: Free, open-source, excellent mobile + web export, smaller than Unity
- Node.js/Socket.io: Proven for real-time games, huge ecosystem
- MongoDB: Flexible schemas, scales well
- Capacitor: Wrap the web game into iOS/Android apps
- PWA: Instant access like Poki, no download needed
