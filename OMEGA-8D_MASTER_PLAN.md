# PROJECT OMEGA-8D
## The Master Blueprint — "The Game They Will Study For Decades"

> "An existential crisis simulator wrapped in a tactical warfare sandbox."

---

## THE THREE LAYERS

### Layer 1: THE TACTICAL MASK
Grounded, hyper-realistic military simulation. Players think they're playing a better Call of Duty.
- Real-world geography, ballistics, squad commands
- 262 weapons across 12 historical eras
- 20 real-world countries for DEFCON infiltration
- Authentic military ranks, units, vehicles
- Sniper team MP, squad operations, killstreaks

### Layer 2: THE PHYSICS BREAK (Mid-Tier Ranks, Level 50+)
The game's physics break open. Higher dimensions become accessible.
- 4D spatial movement (Ana/Kata axis — Adanaxis)
- Time manipulation (rewind, slow-mo, phase, dash)
- Quantum shifting (3 parallel realities)
- Weapons that fire ACROSS dimensions
- Non-Euclidean map topology — space folds, corridors that don't obey geometry

### Layer 3: THE EXISTENTIAL CRISIS (High-Tier + Premium)
Players become dimensional architects. The game stops being a shooter.
- 7D VOID — banish enemies to a void survival instance
- 8D COSMIC — rip enemies to another solar system (mini-survival game to return)
- Permanent map alterations — spatial tears persist across matches
- Players who master dimensions become "Architects" — their exploits scar reality
- The galaxy banishment economy — banished players must scavenge, trade, or pay to return

## KEY MECHANICS (The Mind-Expanders)

### 1. Non-Euclidean Real-World Maps
- Maps overlay on actual global geography (Lagos, Moscow, DC, etc.)
- Coordinate system uses real lat/long scaled to arena size
- Higher-dimensional players FOLD SPACE — a sniper in Lagos can shift the 4th axis
  to bypass a wall and aim through a hyper-dimensional fold at a target across the map
- Non-Euclidean topology: corridors that loop, spaces that are bigger inside,
  rooms that connect through 4D shortcuts
- The map IS the weapon — spatial awareness in 8D is the core skill

### 2. Interstellar Banishment (The Ultimate Troll)
- When a premium 7D/8D player executes a tactical finisher:
  - The enemy isn't killed — they're RIPPED OUT of the match
  - Their avatar is flung into a procedurally generated DEEP SPACE instance
  - This is a mini-survival game: scavenge fuel, fight space creatures, trade resources
  - To return to the firefight, the banished player must:
    a) Scavenge enough fuel to power a warp gate (2-5 minutes)
    b) Trade resources on the micro-economy with other banished players
    c) Pay a coin/token fee to instantly warp back (the "pay to return" option)
    d) Defeat a space guardian boss that guards the warp gate
  - While banished, their squad is down a player — psychological warfare
  - Multiple banished players can meet in the "Deep Space" instance —
    they can team up, trade, or fight each other to steal fuel

### 3. Higher-Dimensional Weapon Physics
- Phase-Shift DMR: Bullet exists in superposition until impact, phases through 3 layers of cover
- Singularity SMG: Fires micro-black-hole rounds that pull enemy cover inward
- Tachyon Sniper: Bullet arrives BEFORE you fire (visual: target takes damage, THEN muzzle flash)
- Void Cannon: Removes a 3m sphere of reality — anything inside ceases to exist for 5s
- Dimensional Rift Gun: Opens a portal between two points, bullets can travel through
- Reality Anchor: Locks a 20m area — NO dimensional abilities work inside (counter-play)
- Every weapon has HYPER-ATTACHMENTS that modify:
  - Gravity interaction (float, heavy, anti-grav)
  - Sound propagation (silent, dimensional echo, disorienting)
  - Spatial behavior (phase through walls, bounce off dimensions, curve trajectory)

### 4. The Architect System
- Players who discover/master higher-dimensional exploits earn "Architect" status
- If a player tears a hole in the map's spatial fabric using 8D abilities:
  - That tear becomes a PERMANENT environmental feature
  - Future matches on that map have the tear — it's a shortcut or hazard
  - The tear bears the creator's gamertag ("RIFT torn by VOIDKING_42")
  - Other players can study, exploit, or seal these tears
  - Maps EVOLVE over time based on player actions — living geography
- The map database tracks all modifications — it's a living record of player warfare
- Top architects get their names on the map loading screen

### 5. Hyper-Attachment System
Every weapon (all 262) has modular attachments that alter dimensional physics:
- Phase Coating: Bullet phases through 1 layer of cover (4D interaction)
- Gravity Mod: Bullet arcs upward or curves around obstacles
- Sound Dampener: Bullet is completely silent (no audio cue)
- Temporal Trigger: Bullet slows time on impact (0.5s slow-mo at hit point)
- Quantum Stabilizer: Bullet hits in all 3 quantum realities simultaneously
- Void Tip: Bullet banishes target to VOID for 3s on hit
- Cosmic Drive: Bullet opens a micro-portal at impact point
- Singularity Core: Bullet creates a 2m gravity well on impact
- Ana/Kata Scope: See enemies in the 4th spatial dimension
- Tachyon Accelerator: Bullet hits 0.2s before you pull the trigger
- Dimensional Echo: Bullet creates a copy in the quantum layer
- Reality Lock: Bullet prevents target from using dimensional abilities for 3s

## THE BANISHMENT ECONOMY

### Deep Space Instance (The "Long Walk Back")
When banished, players enter a procedural deep space survival scenario:
- Procedurally generated asteroid field / dead space station / alien planet
- Must collect 3 FUEL CELLS to power the warp gate
- Fuel cells are guarded by space creatures or hidden in wreckage
- Can TRADE with other banished players (micro-economy):
  - Trade fuel, scrap, dark matter
  - Form temporary alliances
  - Or STEAL from each other (PvP in deep space)
- Can PAY to skip (coin cost scales with how far into the match they are)
- Boss guardian at the warp gate — must defeat to escape
- Time in deep space: 2-5 minutes (or instant with payment)
- While banished: squad is down a player, enemy team has advantage
- Maximum banishment time: 5 minutes (auto-return after)
- The banished player's screen shows the firefight as a "spectator overlay"
  so they can see what they're missing — pure psychological pressure

### Micro-Economy in Deep Space
- Fuel Cell: Tradeable resource, needed for warp gate
- Scrap: Found in wreckage, can be traded
- Dark Matter: Rare, can buy instant warp
- Plasma Crystal: Currency for trading with NPCs
- Other banished players are potential trading partners or enemies

## TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### Current Stack (Prototype)
- Three.js for 3D rendering (browser-based, no download)
- Vanilla JS game engine
- Web Audio API for sound
- Gamepad API for PlayStation/Xbox controller support
- 6D mechanics implemented (4D space + time + quantum)

### Production Stack
- **Game Engine:** Godot 4 (open-source, web + mobile + console export)
  - Supports 3D, physics, networking
  - GDScript for rapid prototyping
  - Can port to PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, mobile, web
- **Backend:** Node.js + Socket.io for real-time multiplayer
- **Database:** MongoDB (player data, map modifications, economy)
- **Map System:** Custom non-Euclidean spatial engine
  - Real-world coordinate system (lat/long → arena space)
  - Persistent map modification database
  - Dimensional tear tracking
- **Banishment Instance:** Procedural generation engine
  - Separate game mode instance
  - Micro-economy trading system
  - Space creature AI
  - Warp gate boss encounters
- **CDN:** Cloudflare for asset delivery
- **Mobile:** Capacitor wrapper for native app stores
- **PWA:** Service worker for instant web access

### Why This Stack
- Godot: Free, open-source, exports everywhere (including PlayStation)
- Custom spatial engine: Needed for non-Euclidean maps
- MongoDB: Flexible schema for living map modifications
- Socket.io: Proven for real-time multiplayer with dimensional sync
